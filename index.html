Splits

Edit a split and everything after it shifts — that is the simulation mode. Edit an elapsed or clock time and the next split absorbs the change, so the finish holds. Arrow keys nudge by 1 s, Shift for 1 min. Drag the handle to reposition a checkpoint; its split and distance clear, since both were measured between its old neighbours.

Every edit is stored in this browser as you make it — the header shows the last save. It does not follow you to another device: “Paste results → Copy backup” gives you the whole thing as JSON, and pasting that JSON back restores it anywhere.
